What it is

A colony-building game about beavers. Chomp trees, dig mud, haul stone and raise a dam across the water. Then hold it: the current never stops wearing at your wall, and the pond only fills if it stands.

  • Every beaver is an individual who gets better at the job they do.
  • Manage the forest: trees you fell must grow back before you can cut again.
  • Storms, floods, droughts, rival clans, trappers and a river monster will test the dam.
  • Each river upstream is wider, faster and stranger.

The browser demo is the first two rivers, free and with no ads. The full game carries the same clan up six hand-built rivers and then endless ones, free with a single ad when you move upstream, or £2.99 once to remove ads for good. No energy timers, no gacha, no accounts, and it plays offline.
